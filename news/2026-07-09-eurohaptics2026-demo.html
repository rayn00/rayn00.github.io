EuroHaptics 2026 で Best Demo Award Honorable Mention を受賞Best Demo Award Honorable Mention at EuroHaptics 2026

2026年7月にイタリア・シエナで開催された触覚の国際会議 EuroHaptics 2026 で、牛山奎悟 特任研究員、佐々尾月陽さん（M2）、雨宮教授のデモ発表が Best Demo Award Honorable Mention を受賞しました。A demo by Project Researcher Keigo Ushiyama, Tsukihi Sasao (M2) and Prof. Amemiya received the Best Demo Award Honorable Mention at EuroHaptics 2026, an international conference on haptics held in Siena, Italy, in July 2026.

両手の腱に振動を与えることで、静止して持っている物体の長さの感じ方が変わることを体験できるデモです。The demo lets visitors experience how vibration applied to the tendons of both hands changes the perceived length of an object held still.

賞Award
Best Demo Award Honorable Mention（EuroHaptics 2026）Best Demo Award Honorable Mention (EuroHaptics 2026)
受賞者Recipients
牛山奎悟（特任研究員）、佐々尾月陽（修士2年）、雨宮智浩（教授）Keigo Ushiyama (Project Researcher), Tsukihi Sasao (M2), Tomohiro Amemiya (Professor)
発表Presentation
Keigo Ushiyama*, Tsukihi Sasao*, Tomohiro Amemiya: “Demonstration of Bimanual Tendon Vibration Alters Perceived Length of a Statically Held Object,” EuroHaptics 2026, Siena, Italy, July 2026.（* 共同第一著者）Keigo Ushiyama*, Tsukihi Sasao*, Tomohiro Amemiya: “Demonstration of Bimanual Tendon Vibration Alters Perceived Length of a Statically Held Object,” EuroHaptics 2026, Siena, Italy, July 2026. (* co-first authors)
